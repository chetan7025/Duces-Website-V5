BUSINESS BANKING SUPPORT

ACCOUNT READINESS

Good preparation starts with clear information.

UAE banks assess each business using their own risk, compliance, activity and eligibility criteria. DUCES helps clients understand common requirements and organise the information requested by a selected institution.

  • Business activity and expected transaction profile
  • Shareholder, ownership and authorised signatory details
  • Source of funds and expected business counterparties
  • Company documents and supporting evidence

TYPICAL DOCUMENTS

Prepare the core company file

Company documents

Trade Licence, Memorandum of Association or equivalent incorporation documents, and establishment or registration details.

Premises evidence

Ejari, lease or other address evidence as requested. Requirements can differ for Free Zone and mainland entities.

Shareholder KYC

Passport, Emirates ID, visa details, contact information and ownership structure for relevant parties.

Business evidence

Contracts, invoices, supplier and customer details, website, business plan or other proof of activity where requested.

Financial information

Expected turnover, transaction volumes, source of funds and available statements or financial records.

Bank-specific forms

Application forms, declarations and additional documents requested by the selected bank.

This is a general checklist, not a guaranteed or exhaustive list. Banks may request additional documents, conduct interviews, decline applications or change requirements without notice.
