MORTGAGE FINANCE

Understand the full property finance picture.

Property finance decisions involve more than the advertised rate. Applicants should review affordability, down payment, valuation, fees, tenure, insurance and early settlement conditions.

  • Applicant income and existing liabilities
  • Property type, location and valuation
  • Deposit and source of funds
  • Tenure, fees and repayment affordability

PREPARATION

Information commonly reviewed

Business finance

Trade Licence, ownership documents, bank statements, VAT returns where applicable, financial statements and business performance evidence.

Mortgage finance

Identity documents, income evidence, bank statements, liabilities, property details and deposit/source-of-funds information.

Affordability review

Existing commitments, repayment capacity, cash flow and lender-specific eligibility conditions.

DUCES does not provide lending, approve credit or guarantee finance. All lending decisions, rates, terms and approvals are determined solely by the relevant financial institution. Independent financial advice may be appropriate before committing.
