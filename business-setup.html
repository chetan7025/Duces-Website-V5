UAE COMPANY FORMATION

CHOOSE YOUR ROUTE

Three company formation routes

The suitable structure depends on your activity, ownership, customers, location, visa needs and operating plans.

Mainland Company

Explore a UAE mainland licence where your planned activity and operating model require access to the wider local market. Activity approvals and ownership conditions vary.

Free Zone Company

Consider a Free Zone structure based on your activity, budget, visa allocation, facility requirements and intended markets. Each authority has its own rules and package conditions.

Offshore Company

Explore offshore incorporation where a holding or international structuring purpose is appropriate. Offshore entities generally do not provide the same UAE operating permissions as an onshore licence.

Setup planning

We help clarify activity selection, shareholder details, licence route, visa requirements and the documentation sequence before an application is submitted.
