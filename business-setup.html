Choosing the Right Setup Route

Starting a business in the United Arab Emirates begins with selecting the correct jurisdiction and legal structure. Your choice depends on your business activity, target clientele, ownership structure, and visa allocations.

01 / Free Zone Company

Ideal for international trade, startups, and consultants seeking 100% foreign ownership, zero personal income tax incentives, and streamlined setup processes across 40+ UAE free zones.

02 / Mainland Company (DED)

Best suited for businesses that require direct trading access to the local UAE market, government contracts, or physical retail storefronts across emirates.

Our End-to-End Setup Process

1. Activity & Jurisdiction Consultation: Evaluating your commercial goals to recommend the most cost-effective licensing structure.
2. Documentation Preparation: Drafting shareholder resolutions, MoAs, and application forms.
3. License Issuance & Visas: Coordinating with government and free zone authorities until your trade license and investor visas are issued.
